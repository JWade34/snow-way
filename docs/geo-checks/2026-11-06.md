## What it tells us

- Snow Way is days old in search indexes, so zero is expected. This is the starting line.
- Small, data-driven sites are being cited (powswirl.com, snowpack.report, snow.watch, snowiest.app). They look a lot like Snow Way: per-resort forecast pages and a "where is it snowing" page. That means the format works; Snow Way needs indexing time and a few links.
- For "where is it snowing," ChatGPT answered worldwide (Whistler, Japan, Scandinavia). Snow Way's page now has an international section, which matches how ChatGPT frames that question.
- Opening dates: snowpack.report's page title includes "(Updated Nov 5)". A visible "Updated" date in the title may help freshness-sensitive answers.
