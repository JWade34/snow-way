**Goal:** show how fun it is to build a real product with AI, from zero to traffic, as a snowboarder who ran a shop and taught himself to code. It does two jobs at once: it grows Snow Way, and it shows people exactly what Justin can build (his consulting audience).

**Series name:** "Snow Way Build Log" (weekly episodes: "Build Log #1", "#2"...). Simple, searchable, easy to binge.

## Where it lives
- **Your personal accounts carry the series** (LinkedIn, X, Instagram @justinmckelvey, YouTube Shorts, TikTok). People follow founders, not tools.
- **@snowway.app stays the product account** (snow reports, storms, tips). It reposts the build log and you tag it.
- **Every link uses a tag** so we can see what works, for example `snowway.app/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=buildlog`. PostHog records the tag on every visit.

## Five things to post about
1. **The why.** Ran a snowboard shop, lived in Vail and Salt Lake, always asking "where's it snowing this weekend?" Your Mammoth Monday story is the origin story.
2. **Build moments.** Real bugs, real fixes, before and after. These are the most fun to watch.
3. **The numbers.** Search Console screenshots, first click, first subscriber, first affiliate dollar. Show it all, including the zeros.
4. **On the mountain.** Testing the forecast in person. "The site said 14 inches at Alta. I went. Here's what it looked like."
5. **Building with AI.** You and Claude building together: what you asked for, what came back, what broke. Show the results, not the instructions.

## The secret sauce line (what stays private)
**Show:** what you built, why, the result, the screens, the numbers, the mistakes, the feeling.
**Don't show:**
- your prompts or skills
- the weekly growth loop and how it decides changes
- keyword lists and which queries we target
- data sources and how the models work (powder odds, rankings)
- affiliate setup and conversion tricks
- the outreach list
Rule of thumb: people should leave knowing **it's possible and fun**, not with a recipe they can copy in an afternoon.

## Weekly rhythm (about 2 hours a week)
| Day | Post | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | The week's numbers (from the Monday growth email) | One screenshot plus 3 lines, on X and LinkedIn |
| Wednesday | One build moment (a bug, a feature, a before and after) | 30 to 45 second screen recording with voiceover, on Reels, Shorts, TikTok and X |
| Friday | **Build Log #N**: what shipped, what you learned, what's next | LinkedIn post or carousel, cut down for X and Instagram |
| Storm days | "The site called it" | Phone video on the mountain or the forecast screen |

If a week gets busy, Friday's Build Log is the one post not to skip.

## First 10 episodes (all from things that already happened)
1. **Why I'm building a ski site.** Shop owner, Vail local, the Monday I didn't go home at Mammoth.
2. **66 to 4,026.** Google impressions went from 66 to 4,026 in 28 days. What changed: hundreds of pages answering real skier questions.
3. **ChatGPT started recommending my site.** Ask it "which ski resort is getting the most snow this week" and it cites Snow Way. Screen recording of the answer.
4. **My iPhone menu sent people to Hotels.com.** A third-party script hijacked every tap. Show the bug, then the fix.
5. **The homepage said 0 inches.** Opened it on my phone, it was ugly and wrong. Before and after redesign.
6. **467 people saw us on Google. Zero clicked.** "What is the best snowboard." How I rebuilt the page (picks from the big reviewers, not my opinion).
7. **10 years of weather for 179 resorts.** "What are the odds of a powder day at your mountain in February?" Show the chart, not the method.
8. **Solo founder outreach.** AI drafts the emails, I read every one and hit send. Why links from real ski sites matter.
9. **First affiliate click, first subscriber.** Small numbers, real milestone.
10. **Testing the forecast in person.** First big storm of the season: go ride it, film it, compare it to the forecast.
